PROJECT 1 / ART 10C

Santa Monica — Uncanny Valley

Inspired by Japanese car magazines, I wanted to reimagine Santa Monica through my own perspective. The ocean, the streets, the cars, and the memories of summer. Everything feels familiar, but somehow different. By mixing images, textures, and typography, I created a version of Santa Monica that exists somewhere between reality and imagination.
